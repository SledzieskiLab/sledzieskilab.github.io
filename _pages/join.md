The Sledzieski Lab at UCSD is currently recruiting across all levels. We are recruiting candidates with computational and/or
biology experience; interdisciplinary training is valued but not required. <b><i>We welcome applications from candidates of all backgrounds,
especially those traditionally under-represented in computing and in the life sciences.</i></b> If you are unsure whether you would be a good
fit for the lab, don't hesitate to email me to ask.

## PhD students

I will be recruiting PhD students to start in Fall 2027. Prospective students should apply through either the
[Halıcıoğlu Data Science Institute](https://datascience.ucsd.edu/graduate/graduate-admissions/) or
[Bioinformatics and Systems Biology](https://bioinformatics.ucsd.edu/admissions/grad/admissions) (BISB) PhD programs.
Please mention my name in your application to be sure that I see it!

**If you are already a UCSD PhD student:** send me an email with `[SL-PHD]` in the subject to discuss whether a position in the lab makes sense.

**PhD Funding Opportunities:**

- [NSF Graduate Research Fellowship Program](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/initiatives/grfp)
- [DOE Computational Science Graduate Fellowship](https://www.krellinst.org/csgf/)
- [Hertz Fellowship](https://www.hertzfoundation.org/hertz-fellowship/)

## Postdocs

I am currently recruiting a computational postdoc to start in Fall 2027. Interested candidates should send me an email
with `[SL-POSTDOC]` in the subject. Please attach your CV, a short description of your research interests, and some representative publications.

**Postdoc Funding Opportunities:**

- [UCSD Guide: Finding Funding Opportunities](https://postdoc.ucsd.edu/funding/finding-funding.html)

- [FutureHouse AI-for-Science Independent Postdoctoral Fellowship](https://www.futurehouse.org/fellowship/overview)
- [NIH K99/R00](https://www.nigms.nih.gov/training/careerdev/Pages/PathwayIndependence)

## Undergraduate and Masters students

We are always looking for motivated undergraduate and masters students that want to gain research experience, often as
part of a thesis project. Please include `[SL-UNDERGRADUATE]` or `[SL-MASTERS]` in the subject, and include a
brief description of the lab project(s) that you are interested in. Prior research experience is not required.
